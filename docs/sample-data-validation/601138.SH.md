## Unverified items

- Current official listing-status field and delisting/status history through a documented API.
- Licensed/cacheable structured balance sheet, income statement and cash-flow fields.
- Restatement/version identifiers for structured A-share financial facts.
- Historical adjustment factors and a complete normalized corporate-action feed.
- Automated CNINFO API terms, caching, display and redistribution permissions.
- Provider completeness for the required three annual and four discrete quarters.

## Data gaps and risks

1. Public SSE JSON/JSONP endpoints demonstrate technical reachability but not a production contract.
2. PDF parsing alone is insufficient for production financial normalization and is vulnerable to layout changes.
3. Main-statement units are thousands of RMB, while other annual-report tables use yuan, ten-thousand yuan, hundred-million yuan or shares.
4. A-share cumulative quarters make naive TTM calculations wrong.
5. Dividend and share changes are visible in filings, but price adjustment requires a complete corporate-action timeline.

## Required before formal integration

- Select and authenticate a structured A-share provider whose personal-use license permits local caching.
- Obtain written/provider-page confirmation for API rate, historical coverage, corrections and storage/display terms.
- Reconcile provider facts against the official 2025 annual and 2025 Q3 PDFs.
- Validate adjustment factors and corporate actions across at least one ex-dividend/share-change event.
- Preserve the official PDF as source-of-truth evidence even when structured data is used.
