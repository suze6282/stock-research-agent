# Structured Research Packages

A Research Package organizes Claim IDs, Evidence IDs, unsupported and
conflicting Claims, blocked capabilities, data quality, limitations, and
warnings. Section states are `PASS`, `PARTIAL`, `BLOCKED`, `NO_EVIDENCE`, or
`NOT_REQUESTED`.

Packages do not contain a prose stock report, rating, target price, position
size, return forecast, or trade instruction. Empty sections are explicit and
never filled with invented content.

Synthetic engineering packages may be COMPLETE only when marked
`SYNTHETIC_TEST_ONLY`, `NOT_COMPANY_EVIDENCE`, `OFFLINE`, and `NOT_LIVE`.
Industrial FII and Micron packages remain `PARTIAL` or `BLOCKED` until verified
company bodies and facts exist.
